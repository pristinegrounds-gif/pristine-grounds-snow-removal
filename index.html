Common Questions

Before the first storm.

What is your snowfall trigger?

Standard automatic service begins after approximately 1 inch, unless a different trigger is agreed to for the property.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. Seasonal and per-push options are available. The exact arrangement depends on the property and route capacity.

Do you offer one-time snow removal?

Sometimes. Seasonal clients are serviced first. One-time work is accepted only as capacity allows and may cost more during major storms.

Do you provide salting?

Yes, selectively. Salting makes the most sense when several customers on the route require it.
